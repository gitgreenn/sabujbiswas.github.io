Building MyLLM: Inside Tokenization, Embeddings & Attention

What happens under the hood when constructing an autoregressive transformer model from scratch? Deconstructing matrix dimensions, causal masking, and loss calculation.

01 // MOTIVATION

Why Build from Scratch?

Modern frameworks often obscure how neural networks process textual tokens. Building a minimal transformer from first principles illuminates how mathematical representations emerge from discrete text without magical black-box abstractions.

02 // MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION

The Self-Attention Matrix

The core of modern LLMs is Scaled Dot-Product Attention:

Attention Equation 
Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax( (Q * K^T) / sqrt(d_k) ) * V

In autoregressive generation, we must enforce causality by masking upper-triangular future tokens with negative infinity prior to softmax, ensuring the model never cheats by seeing tokens ahead of the current timestep.
